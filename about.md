# about

<p class="lede">Self-taught cook and filmmaker. Twelve years spent making things
that make food better — on screen, in print, and in the hand.</p>

I make **obsessive food series** on YouTube. One subject at a time, taken further than
is reasonable: the perfect fries, the perfect pizza, the machine nobody makes because
nobody needs it. The channel has passed two million subscribers. It started as
*French Guy Cooking*; it has been called *Alex* for years now.

The same obsession produced **a book** — *Just a French Guy Cooking* (Quadrille, 2018) —
and **SALUT**, a copper cookware company I founded to make the pans I could not find.
I am also a recurring contributor to **Milk Street**.

## Before

Trained as an engineer. Then photo assistant at **Magnum Photos** and **Agence VU**,
which is where I learned to look at things. Then a digital marketing agency, which is
where I learned to make them land. The engineering never left: it is why a video about
a frying pan ends up being about heat transfer.

## What I do for other people

Creative direction, for twelve years and counting — brand films, product storytelling,
and the awkward space between a good object and the story that makes people want it.
Design, film, and the engineering underneath are the same job to me.

## Elsewhere

Cycling, dancing, and sailing take the rest. They have nothing to do with food, which
is exactly the point.
